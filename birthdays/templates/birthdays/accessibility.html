Our commitment

GiftMe is committed to improving accessibility across our marketing pages, account flows, and celebration experiences. We believe meaningful gifting should not depend on how someone sees, hears, moves, or processes information.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We review new features with inclusion in mind and welcome feedback from the people who use GiftMe every day.

Standards we follow

We aim to conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA where practical. Our work includes:

  • Semantic HTML structure with headings, landmarks, and descriptive labels.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation for primary actions and interactive components.
  • Visible focus states and sufficient color contrast for text and controls.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images and decorative images marked appropriately.
  • Form fields associated with clear labels and error messages.
  • Support for screen readers through ARIA attributes where native semantics are not enough.

What we are doing

Recent and ongoing accessibility improvements include:

  • Skip-friendly page structure with labeled navigation regions in the header and footer.
  • Responsive layouts that reflow on smaller screens without loss of content or function.
  • Reduced reliance on color alone to convey important information.
  • Testing keyboard paths through sign-up, dashboard, and public gift page flows.
  • Reviewing motion and animation so essential content remains readable when motion is reduced.

Known limitations

Some areas of GiftMe may not yet meet our accessibility goals. Examples may include:

  • User-uploaded images on celebration pages, which may lack alternative text unless provided by the page creator.
  • Third-party payment or embed experiences governed by external accessibility practices.
  • Legacy dashboard components that we are gradually improving.

We prioritize fixes that remove barriers to core tasks such as creating a page, sharing a link, and making a contribution.

Third-party content

GiftMe may link to or integrate content from social platforms, payment providers, and illustration libraries. We cannot fully control the accessibility of those external services, but we choose partners carefully and advocate for inclusive experiences wherever we can.

Feedback and assistance

If you encounter a barrier while using GiftMe, or if you need information in an alternative format, please tell us. We will do our best to respond promptly and work toward a solution.

When you contact us, it helps to include:

  • The page or feature you were using.
  • What you were trying to do.
  • The assistive technology or browser you use, if applicable.
